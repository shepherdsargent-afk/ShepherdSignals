ShepherdSignals Automation Guide

Full Automation Setup

Everything that happens automatically — from a lead visiting the site to Shepherd's phone ringing and the client accessing their audit.

Zero manual work Stripe · Zapier · Calendly · Twilio ~2 hours to set up once

How it works

The complete automated pipeline

1

Lead visits the site, fills out audit request form

The "Book free audit" form on website-sample.html captures name, club, email, and phone. On submit, it fires a webhook to Zapier in the background.

website-sample.html Webhook → Zapier
2

Shepherd's phone gets an instant SMS

Within seconds of form submission, Twilio sends Shepherd a text: "New lead: [Name], [Club], [email], [phone]. Reply to call them now." No app to check. No email to miss.

Zapier → Twilio SMS ~30 seconds from form to phone
3

Client receives a calendar booking link automatically

Zapier also sends the client a Calendly link via email: "Shepherd will reach out within 24 hours. In the meantime, book a call at a time that works for you." The call lands in Shepherd's calendar automatically.

Zapier → Gmail → Client Calendly booking widget
4

Client pays → portal unlocks automatically

When the client pays via Stripe, Stripe sends a webhook to Zapier. Zapier immediately sends the client an email with their portal link and a unique access token. No manual invoice, no manual follow-up.

Stripe Webhook → Zapier Automated welcome email portal.html access
5

Shepherd's phone confirms the payment

A second Twilio SMS fires when payment is confirmed: "Payment received: [Club], [Plan], $[Amount]. They've been sent the portal link. Reach out to welcome them within 24 hours."

Stripe → Zapier → Twilio
6

Email sequence runs on autopilot

For cold leads (non-openers, non-payers), Mailchimp or ConvertKit runs the 6-email funnel automatically — Day 1, 3, 6, 10, 14, 21. Anyone who pays gets removed from the sequence automatically via a Zapier trigger.

Mailchimp / ConvertKit Auto-remove on payment

Step-by-step setup

What to set up and where

Stripe
Payments · ~30 min to set up
1
Create account at stripe.com
2
Go to Products → Payment Links → Create two links: Monthly ($649/mo) and Annual ($6,588/yr)
3
Copy both links into portal.html where it says STRIPE_PAYMENT_LINK_MONTHLY and _ANNUAL
4
In Stripe Dashboard → Developers → Webhooks → Add endpoint: your Zapier webhook URL. Event: payment_intent.succeeded
5
Add your bank account for payouts (Settings → Bank accounts)
⏱ ~30 minutes · No code needed
Zapier
Automation hub · ~45 min total
1
Create account at zapier.com (free plan covers basic use)
2
Zap 1: Trigger = Webhooks (new form submission) → Action = Twilio (send SMS to Shepherd) + Gmail (send calendar link to client)
3
Zap 2: Trigger = Stripe (new payment) → Action = Gmail (send portal access email to client) + Twilio (notify Shepherd)
4
Zap 3: Trigger = Stripe (new payment) → Action = Mailchimp/ConvertKit (remove from cold sequence)
⏱ ~45 minutes · No code · Free up to 100 tasks/month
Twilio
SMS to Shepherd's phone · ~20 min
1
Sign up at twilio.com → Get a free trial number
2
Add $20 credit (lasts months at low volume)
3
Note your Account SID, Auth Token, and Twilio phone number
4
In Zapier, connect Twilio with these credentials. Set "To" = Shepherd's mobile number
5
Test with a dummy form submission — you should get a text within 30 seconds
⏱ ~20 minutes · ~$0.0075 per SMS
Calendly
Client call booking · ~15 min
1
Create account at calendly.com (free plan works)
2
Create an event type: "ShepherdSignals Discovery Call" — 30 min, phone or Zoom
3
Set your availability (block out mornings for golf club time zones — they're early people)
4
Copy your Calendly link — paste it into the Zapier "send calendar email" action
5
Enable email reminders in Calendly (24hr and 1hr before the call)
⏱ ~15 minutes · Free plan sufficient

Cold email delivery

Setting up the email funnel

📧
Step 1 — Platform
Sign up for Mailchimp (free up to 500 contacts)
mailchimp.com → Create account → Create new Audience: "Golf Clubs Cold Outreach"
→
Step 2 — Import
Upload your golf club contacts CSV
Columns: First Name, Last Name, Club Name, Email, City, Province/Country, Primary Supplier (if known)
→
Step 3 — Sequence
Create a Customer Journey (automation) in Mailchimp
6 emails: Day 1, +2 days, +3 days, +4 days, +4 days, +7 days. Copy text from email-funnel.html into each step.
→
Step 4 — Remove on payment
Connect Zapier to auto-remove paying clients from the cold sequence
Zapier: Trigger = Stripe payment → Action = Mailchimp: unsubscribe or move to "Clients" audience. They never receive another cold email.

Building the list

How to find golf club GMs worldwide

🌐 Free Sources
1
NGCOA Canada — National Golf Course Owners Association. ngcoa.ca has member directories
2
Golf Canada — gcgolf.ca lists affiliated clubs with contact details
3
LinkedIn — Search "General Manager golf club" + filter by country. 85%+ have email findable via Hunter.io
4
Google Maps — "golf club" + city → website → "Contact Us" → GM email usually listed
💰 Paid Sources (faster)
1
Hunter.io ($49/mo) — Find verified emails for any golf club domain in seconds
2
Apollo.io ($49/mo) — Filter by industry "Golf Clubs & Courses", title "General Manager". Export 1000s of contacts
3
Clay.earth — AI-powered lead enrichment. Import club names, it finds GMs + emails automatically
4
Instantly.ai — Cold email sending platform with warmup + deliverability built in
